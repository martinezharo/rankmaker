# RANKMAKER

RANKMAKER is a web app for turning preferences into an ordered ranking through one-versus-one comparisons. Instead of placing many items into broad tier-list buckets, the user repeatedly chooses between two options and RANKMAKER builds the ordered result.

## Core experience

- Browse official and community ranking templates or create a custom template with 4 to 50 options.
- Rank options through head-to-head battles with undo, skip, early finish, battle history, and manual result reordering.
- Run eligible guest templates locally without creating an account.
- Share template links and finished rankings or download a result image.
- Discover templates by search and category and see how many times a template has been ranked.

## Creator and community features

Signed-in users can create public, unlisted, or private templates. Public community features include creator profiles, follows, saved templates, comments, and votes. Template creators can request an AI-assisted description suggestion. The interface supports English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, and Malay; user-created and official template content may remain in its original language.

## Good fit

RANKMAKER is useful when someone wants a personal, ordered answer to questions such as favorite films, albums, games, foods, teams, or characters and finds direct comparisons easier than sorting a full list at once.

It is a preference-ranking tool, not an objective rating authority or a polling service. A ranking represents the choices made in that run, and finishing early or manually reordering the result can affect its precision.
